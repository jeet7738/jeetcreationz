Privacy Policy - BrainX : Live Quiz & GK Test

Last updated: October 2026

1. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliant)

BrainX is strictly family-friendly and 100% compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not collect any personal identifiable information (PII) from children.

2. Google AdMob Services

We use Google AdMob to serve child-directed ads. AdMob is configured with strict COPPA parameters (TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT_TRUE) to ensure no personalized tracking or sensitive ads are displayed.

3. Offline Data Persistence

All your quiz scores, coin balance, and settings are saved 100% locally on your device using Jetpack DataStore. Your data never leaves your device.

4. Google Play Compliance & Data Safety

Our app adheres strictly to Google Play Developer Program Policies regarding data collection, safety, and security. No user data is shared with third parties for tracking purposes.

For any questions or support regarding privacy, feel free to contact us at jeetcreation03@gmail.com.
