Privacy Policy for StatusX

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This privacy policy explains how StatusX ("we," "our," or "us") handles your information. We're committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices. All data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

1. How Our App Works

StatusX helps you discover, view, and manage status updates from social media platforms. The app operates entirely on your device with no server-side data storage.

2. Data Handling & Local Storage

All app data, including saved media and preferences, is stored locally on your device. We do not have any servers that store your personal information or media files. To delete saved media or reset data, use your device's file manager or app settings.

3. Premium Purchase

We offer premium features through Google Play's one-time in-app purchase system. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play; we do not collect or store payment information.

4. Advertising (Free Version)

The free version includes ads served exclusively through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, and usage data.

5. Permissions

Our app requires storage access to scan for, save, and manage status media, and internet access for displaying ads and enabling in-app purchases.

6. Your Privacy Controls

Manage app permissions in your device settings, opt out of personalized ads through your device's advertising settings, or contact Google Play Support for purchase management.

7. Changes to This Policy & Contact Us

We may update this policy from time to time. If you have any questions, please contact us at jeetcreation03@gmail.com.
