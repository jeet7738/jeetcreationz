Privacy Policy - ResizeX : Image Resizer

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ResizeX. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

2. Data We Collect & Offline Processing

ResizeX is designed to prioritize user privacy. Your images and documents are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any external servers.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All financial transactions, in-app purchases, and subscription payments are processed securely directly by Google Play Billing Services. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card details or banking credentials.

4. Permissions

We request access to your photo library solely to allow you to select images for processing and to save exported results back to your gallery.

5. Advertisements

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements for free users. Google may use device identifiers to serve relevant ads. Pro users enjoy a 100% ad-free experience.

6. Children's Privacy

ResizeX does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
